SAMPLE FUNDING ANNOUNCEMENT (based on the public 2026 Grand Challenges Annual Meeting Call-to-Action rules)

Eligibility
Participants registered for the 2026 Grand Challenges Annual Meeting may submit one application as the Primary Applicant on behalf of an eligible affiliated organization. Applications are welcome from nonprofit organizations, for-profit companies, international organizations, government agencies, and academic institutions. Individuals are not eligible to receive funding. Collaborative pilot proposals must involve participants from multiple institutions.

Awards
We will consider proposals for awards of up to $100,000 USD for individual pilot projects, or $200,000 for collaborative pilot projects, with a grant term of 18 months. Indirect costs are allowable and must be included within the total requested funding.

Required Documents
Your application includes a proposal (maximum 2 pages), a budget table and narrative (1 page maximum, template provided), and a collaboration statement if the project is collaborative (1 page maximum). Do not include a cover sheet.

Proposal Format
The proposal must be no longer than 2 pages. Figures and references count towards the page limit. Use 11-point font or larger, at least 0.5 inch margins all around, and single line spacing. Use Aptos, Arial, or Times New Roman font. Submit as a Microsoft Word or Adobe PDF file of 3MB or less.

Deadline
Applications are due no later than July 9, 2026, 11:30 a.m. U.S. Pacific Time.
